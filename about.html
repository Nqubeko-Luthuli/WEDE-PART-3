About Us

Our history

Ubuntu Paws Rescue was founded in 2016 by a small group of Johannesburg volunteers. Today we run two shelters and have rehomed over 1,200 dogs and cats.

Mission and vision

  • Mission: Rescue, rehabilitate and rehome abandoned animals.
  • Vision: A community where every animal is safe and loved.
